medical_services Project Overview

An intelligent Voice agent for health care clinic designed to handle patient inquiries, appointment scheduling, and basic medical triage autonomously.

Built with a modern stack including Next.js and TypeScript, the agent uses Retell AI for low-latency, natural voice synthesis and recognition. The complex backend logic and scheduling workflows are managed by n8n, ensuring seamless integration with clinic management systems.
